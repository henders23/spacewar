3 · How the campaign works

Scars carry over

Hull damage, criticals and crew losses stay with a ship until repaired at a drydock. A crippled ship can still fly the next mission, or rest in dock and miss it.

Lost is lost

Destroyed or captured ships are gone, and so are their captains, upgrades and veterancy. Disengaging off the map edge saves the ship but may fail the mission.

Prizes

A ship you capture by boarding joins your fleet with its surviving prize crew and flies with you from the next mission. It needs crew and repairs, and has fewer refit slots.

Veterancy

Green → Trained (1 battle) → Veteran (3: re-roll one 1 per volley) → Elite (6: boarders win ties). Shown on each ship; veterancy and captain traits are not in the battle rules yet.
